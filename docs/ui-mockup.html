Sparse-View CBCT Reconstruction with Implicit Neural Representations
1 Introduction

Cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) is widely used in dental and image-guided interventions because of its compact geometry and low cost. Reducing the number of projections lowers the radiation dose, but standard FDK reconstruction then suffers from severe streak artifacts.

Implicit neural representations (INRs) model the attenuation field as a continuous function and have shown promise for sparse-view settings. However, existing methods optimise each scan from scratch and ignore the consistency between neighbouring projections.

Fig. 1. Reconstructions from 20 projections. Left: FDK. Middle: NAF. Right: ours.

In this work we propose a projection-consistent neural field that couples a hash-encoded INR with a differentiable cone-beam projector, and regularises the field with a cross-view consistency term.

1 引言

锥形束 CT（CBCT）结构紧凑、成本低，被广泛用于口腔和影像引导介入。减少投影数量可以降低辐射剂量，但此时标准 FDK 重建会出现严重的条状伪影。

隐式神经表示（INR）把衰减场建模为连续函数，在稀疏视角场景下表现出潜力。然而，现有方法对每次扫描都从零开始优化，忽略了相邻投影之间的一致性。

本文提出一种投影一致的神经场：将哈希编码的 INR 与可微分锥束投影器结合，并用跨视角一致性项对场进行正则化。

为什么投影少了 FDK 就会出条状伪影？
AIDeepSeek · High

FDK 是解析重建，默认投影角度足够密。角度变稀疏后，反投影时每个方向的贡献没法相互抵消，残留下来就成了沿投影方向的条纹。

论文用两点来缓解：

  1. 用连续的神经场代替离散体素，在没有投影的角度上靠场的平滑性补全；
  2. 加一个跨视角一致性损失，约束相邻投影的预测结果。
